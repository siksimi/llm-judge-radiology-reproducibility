RUBRIC (score each item 1-5; 5 = excellent, 1 = poor; apply anchors exactly):

1) overall_global_quality  (rate holistically; NOT the average of the other items)
   5 = Excellent; comparable to a high-quality radiologist report; usable as-is.
   4 = Good; minor edits only.
   3 = Acceptable; moderate revision needed.
   2 = Poor; substantial revision needed.
   1 = Unacceptable; not usable.

2) clinical_appropriateness
   5 = Impression and any recommendations are clinically apt and actionable; appropriate to the indication.
   4 = Largely appropriate; minor gaps in emphasis or recommendation.
   3 = Adequate but with questionable relevance or a weak/missing recommendation.
   2 = Clinically misaligned; inappropriate emphasis or a missing necessary recommendation.
   1 = Clinically inappropriate or potentially harmful guidance.

3) clarity_structure
   5 = Clear, well-organized, concise; standard structure; no redundancy.
   4 = Mostly clear; minor organization or redundancy issues.
   3 = Understandable but notably disorganized, verbose, or ambiguous.
   2 = Hard to follow; poor structure or significant ambiguity.
   1 = Incoherent or unintelligible.

4) diagnostic_accuracy
   5 = All statements supported and correct; no hallucinated findings; laterality and measurements correct.
   4 = Trivial inaccuracy without clinical impact.
   3 = One noticeable error (a hallucinated finding OR a laterality/measurement error) with limited impact.
   2 = Multiple errors, or one clinically significant error affecting diagnosis.
   1 = Pervasive hallucinations/errors; diagnostically unreliable.

5) finding_completeness
   5 = All clinically relevant findings reported; nothing omitted.
   4 = Only a minor, non-critical finding missing; interpretation unaffected.
   3 = A relevant finding omitted; some impact but main message intact.
   2 = Important finding(s) omitted; interpretation compromised.
   1 = Major/critical findings missing; report largely incomplete.
